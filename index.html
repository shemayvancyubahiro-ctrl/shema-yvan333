About Us

GS Kigeme A is a secondary school managed by the Anglican Church of Rwanda, Kigeme Diocese, together with the Government of Rwanda, and it sits close to the main road from Kigali to Butare and Cyangugu. The school began in 1965 and grew step by step into Groupe Scolaire Kigeme in 1990. It started with girls only and later became co-educational. Today it offers the ordinary level and three combinations: MCB, PCB and MPC and streams levels(MS1&MS2 S).
