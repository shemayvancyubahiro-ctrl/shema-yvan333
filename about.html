Who We Are

GS KIGEME A

GS KIGEME A is a secondary school managed by the Anglican Church of Rwanda, Kigeme Diocese, together with the Government of Rwanda.

The school is located in Rwanda and has a long history of contributing to the education and development of young people.

Our school provides education that helps students develop academic knowledge, discipline, creativity, leadership and practical skills.
