WHO WE ARE

Welcome to GS KIGEME A

GS KIGEME A is a learning community dedicated to helping students develop academically, socially and personally.

We believe that education is more than classroom lessons. It is an opportunity for learners to discover their talents, develop confidence, build character and prepare for meaningful contributions to society.

Through the commitment of our teachers, students, families and school community, we continue to create an environment where learners are encouraged to learn, grow and aim for excellence.
